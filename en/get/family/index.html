One subscription for all five games

The shared content subscription unlocks the learning content in Flags, Math, English, the Robot and Riddle Ladder while it is active. In the other games, sign in with the same parent account. Prices and terms are shown in the app before purchase.

Every game starts free: 5 journeys in Flags, 5 stages in Math, 5 topics in English and 2 worlds in the Robot. Riddle Ladder also starts free — coming soon. The rest is paid: a monthly subscription that renews automatically and can be cancelled at any time, and in Flag Ladder also a one-time purchase. The price is shown in the app before purchase, for your Apple account.
