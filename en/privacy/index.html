Privacy Policy

This policy applies to all three Ladder Learning Games on iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV.

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Information inside the apps

The developer does not collect, share, or sell personal information inside the apps. They contain no advertising, usage analytics, tracking, or developer-managed user account.

Website and beta waitlist

The static website does not use user accounts, advertising cookies, or analytics. The beta form does not send information to the website's server; it prepares a message in the user's email app. If you choose to send it, the developer receives your name, email address, selected games and devices, and the optional note you entered.

The information is used only to manage the waitlist, send TestFlight invitations, communicate with testers, and address feedback. It is not sold or used for targeted advertising.

Progress and settings

Progress, settings, and character selection are stored on the device. When iCloud is available, this data may synchronize through Apple's iCloud service between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer does not operate a server that receives this data and cannot access it.

Apple services

iCloud, Game Center, the App Store, and TestFlight are Apple services. The apps use them for progress synchronization, achievements, purchases, and beta testing according to the user's choices. Apple's handling of information is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Purchases and subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive card numbers, passwords, or payment details. Apple provides the app only with the technical information required to verify entitlement to purchased content.

Children

The apps do not ask children for a name, email address, location, photos, or other personal details, and they do not include chat or user-generated content.

Retention and deletion

Deleting an app removes its locally stored data. Data synchronized through iCloud, Game Center achievements, and purchase history are managed through the Apple Account and can be controlled using Apple's settings and services.

Registration details and feedback sent by email are retained only as long as needed to manage the beta, respond to a request, and track a repair. They are deleted when no longer needed. You may ask at any time to remove your registration details or stop further contact.

Privacy requests
