Choose a monthly subscription for Riddle Ladder alone or the family plan for all five games.

Thinking, discovering, and understanding

Thinking riddles for children aged 7–8, offering familiarity with the kinds of riddles found in gifted-children screening. The game is not affiliated with the Ministry of Education and contains no real test questions. It is for practice and enrichment and does not guarantee any screening result.

Monthly subscriptions and restoration

Start with free content. There are two monthly subscriptions: Riddle Ladder alone, or the family plan covering Flag Ladder, Math Ladder, English Ladder, The Confused Robot, and Riddle Ladder. Purchases are made through Apple only and are protected by a parent gate. Apple shows the billing and renewal terms before confirmation. Access to subscription content lasts while the subscription is active.

To restore access, sign in to the Apple Account used for the purchase and choose “Restore Purchases” in the app. For family access in the other games, also sign in with the same parent account in each game. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Account settings. Deleting the app or parent account does not cancel a subscription.

Optional shared parent account

The five games share an optional parent account using Sign in with Apple. You can start playing and purchase without a parent account. To use the family subscription across the games, sign in with the same parent account in each one. Download the other games separately. Account deletion is available from the parent-account screen; contact support if you need help.

Recorded explanations, available offline

The game reads explanations aloud from recordings bundled inside the app. Playback needs no internet connection and sends no text, audio, or other information anywhere.

Game Center achievements

Sign in to Game Center in device settings to use achievements. Achievements are awarded when their in-game requirements are met; offline progress may be reported after reconnecting.

For parents

The game has a parent gate, no ads, and no tracking. Purchases and the optional parent account are for adults. The same privacy practices apply across all five games.
