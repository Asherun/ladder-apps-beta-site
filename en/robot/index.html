Learn, discover, and keep moving

A curiosity game for children: answer questions about science, nature, and the world, read or hear a short explanation, and travel between the factory wings. A mistake is a chance to learn and try again. Along the way, collect coins and dress up the robot.

Subscription and restoration

The first two worlds are free. The monthly subscription unlocks every world while it is active. The price, billing period, and renewal terms shown in the app come directly from the App Store for your Apple Account's region. Existing permanent Robot purchases remain supported.

You can also choose one subscription for all four apps: flags, math, English, and the robot. An optional parent account opens it in the other games too.

To restore, sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase and choose Restore Purchases. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Game Center and achievements

Sign in to Game Center in the device settings. Achievements are awarded for completing worlds and for perfect journeys, and progress earned offline may be sent after you sign in.
