Before you start

How do I get access to the beta?

After your request is reviewed and approved, a personal TestFlight invitation is sent to the Apple Account email you provided. There is no public signup link. You can send feedback through TestFlight after playing.

Do beta purchases cost anything?

Purchases within the TestFlight version are not charged. A local price may appear to test the purchase flow, but Apple marks it as a test transaction. Purchases and subscriptions in the regular App Store version are charged under the terms shown by Apple.

What should I include in my feedback?

The game, platform, device model, system version, and what happened on screen. Do not include children's details, photos, or recordings. You can play together, but the invitation, account, and feedback remain with the adult. Read our Privacy Policy for more.
