Before you play

How does the four-app subscription work?

The shared content subscription unlocks learning content in Flags, Math, English, and the Robot while it is active. To use it in the other games, sign in with the same parent account. Prices and terms are shown in the app before purchase.

Can we start without a subscription?

Yes. The first five journeys, levels, or themes in the Ladder games are free, as are the Robot’s first two worlds. No sign-in is needed for the free content. Additional content is available through in-app purchases.

Which devices can we play on?

All four games are available on iPhone and iPad. Flag Ladder, Math Ladder, and English Ladder are also available on Apple TV.
